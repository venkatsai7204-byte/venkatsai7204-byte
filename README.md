## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Customer Support Representative (L1) & Client Assist** based in **Hyderabad**, with **3.9+ years of experience** in health insurance process management for US clients. I provide real-time chat and email support to clients and brokers, and manage enrollments, demographic updates, and terminations across the client portal — **Anthem Blue Cross, Blue Shield, California Choice, Kaiser, and United Healthcare**.

I handle open enrollment activities, process changes, and daily stakeholder updates while maintaining accuracy and compliance with U.S. healthcare regulations. I also monitor and audit employee profiles across client platform to catch data integrity issues before they become processing errors, and I've contributed to internal process and communication improvements that made the service model more efficient and customer-centric.

Outside of my current role, I completed my **B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering**, where I worked on IoT and image-processing based systems (see Projects below).

**🎯 What I'm looking for:** **Data Analytics** roles, along with positions that build on my background in process compliance, data accuracy, and client support operations.

**⚡ Strengths:** Detail-oriented and fast learner · Strong communication & interpersonal skills · Highly adaptable and team-oriented

**🎨 Hobbies:** Watching movies · Playing cricket · Browsing the internet to learn new things and improve my knowledge
